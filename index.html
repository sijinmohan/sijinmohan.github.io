06 / Approach

How I operate.

Five years of running other people's budgets, and my own, boiled down to six rules.

  • 01

    Revenue is the scoreboard

    CAC, ROAS, LTV and revenue. Reach and likes only count when they move those.

  • 02

    Measure before you scale

    Tracking comes first. If a sale can't be traced to a campaign, that gets fixed before more money goes in.

  • 03

    Spend it like it's mine

    I've run companies on my own money. Every rupee of a client's budget gets the same scrutiny.

  • 04

    Test fast, cut faster

    Structured creative and funnel experiments with clear kill rules, so budget only follows what works.

  • 05

    Build systems, not dependence

    Dashboards, playbooks and processes a team can run without me in the room.

  • 06

    Straight answers

    Honest reporting, including what didn't work, why, and what we'll do next.

  1. Every engagement
  2. 01AuditAccounts, tracking and funnel. Where is money leaking?
  3. 02PlanChannels, budgets, tests and the one target that matters.
  4. 03LaunchCampaigns and tracking live, with a dashboard from day one.
  5. 04ScaleMore budget only behind what's proven to work.
